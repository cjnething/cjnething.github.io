Overview

Independently designed, built, and deployed a handbag discovery platform with advanced attribute search, comparison, analytics, and an outreach-driven launch. Shoppers still buy on the designer’s site; Acquired Taste surfaces the decision details retailers bury in product copy.

Built end-to-end—product definition, data model, ingest/curator tooling, shopper UX, deployment, and iteration from real user feedback—while using AI as a development and ingest assistant, not as a source of invented metadata.

Visit Acquired Taste

How it evolved

I originally set out to build a site where people could find clothes or accessories by a specific color—mustard yellow, for example, instead of just “yellow.” Over time that felt too broad, so I narrowed to a problem I’d hit many times myself: finding the right bag. Once I focused there, the product expanded well past color. I wanted to search by anything I could name—strap drop, laptop fit, hardware, pockets, shipping—and not just about the bags themselves. People also shop based on the company: BIPOC-owned, B Corp, sustainably made, and so on. Whatever actually decides the purchase.

Problem

People often already know what they want—“red leather crossbody with silver hardware and a back pocket”—but retail search can’t express it. Useful specs (laptop fit, key leash, strap drop, shipping destinations) exist in copy and can’t be filtered. The same is true for brand-level choices: whether a company is BIPOC-owned, a B Corp, or sustainably made is often buried on an About page, if it’s findable at all. Independent brands lose shoppers who give up before finding them.

What shipped

  • Attribute-first browse — facet search over structured bag specs and brand attributes (ownership, certifications, sustainability)—not visual similarity as the primary loop
  • Quiz → shortlist — guided discovery that narrows to a manageable set of bags
  • Compare — side-by-side decision details across candidates
  • Curator / brand admin — URL ingest → AI pre-fill → human edit → verify → publish
  • Analytics — PostHog instrumentation for shopper funnels, outbound clicks, and waitlist/brand leads
  • Production deploy — HTTPS web + API + PostgreSQL on a public domain, with waitlist and brand outreach paths

Stack

  • Web: Next.js, React, TypeScript
  • API: FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Alembic
  • Data: PostgreSQL 16
  • Infra: Docker; hosted production (Vercel + API/DB)

Where we are now

I’ve curated a small set of independent handbag designers with the kind of details I think shoppers actually want to know—structured attributes, not just pretty photos. I’ve also started contacting those designers for feedback, and to ask whether they’d be open to me featuring their designs on the site.

Why it belongs here

The academic projects on this site show research foundations—learning science, HCI, and information design. Acquired Taste is the shipping counterpart: a product I conceived, built, deployed, and am actively improving in the real world.
